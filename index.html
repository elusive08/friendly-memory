How a check-in becomes care

A simple SMS loop connects every registered mother to the health worker responsible for her, however far she is from the clinic.

01

Register once

A health extension worker enrolls a pregnant woman with her phone number and due date — on a feature phone or a smartphone, it doesn't matter.

02

Daily SMS check-in

MamaCheck texts her simple questions about how she's feeling and reminders for upcoming antenatal appointments, timed to her stage of pregnancy.

03

Feedback reaches her worker

Her replies are logged and scored. Anything concerning is flagged as a red alert so her assigned worker can follow up right away.

Why plain SMS, not an app

Most maternal health apps assume a smartphone and steady data. In the communities MamaCheck reaches, that assumption leaves women out.

No app or internet needed

Every message travels as plain SMS, so it reaches basic phones and works even where data coverage is unreliable.

Built around her stage of pregnancy

Questions and reminders adjust week by week, so a first-trimester check-in never reads like a third-trimester one.

Concerning answers surface fast

Replies that suggest a warning sign are flagged as red alerts, so her health worker can act the same day, not at the next scheduled visit.
